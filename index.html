Beginner's Guide to Volleyball

🏐

Basic Rules

  • Two teams of six players
  • Rally scoring (first to 25 points)
  • Maximum of three touches per side
  • Rotate positions after winning the serve

Volleyball Equipment

  • Volleyball
  • Net and posts
  • Knee pads
  • Volleyball court

Basic Techniques

  • Serving (underhand and overhand)
  • Passing (bump)
  • Setting
  • Spiking and blocking
